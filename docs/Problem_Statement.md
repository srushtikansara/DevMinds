## Problem statement:
Developers and AI learners work across multiple tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, GitHub, VS Code, terminals, and documentation throughout their learning and development process. However, their conversations, coding history, problem-solving approaches, and learning progress remain fragmented across these platforms. As a result, they often forget previously learned concepts, struggle to recall past solutions, cannot accurately track their skill growth, and spend significant time identifying what to learn next or preparing for career opportunities. There is a need for a unified developer intelligence tool that continuously captures learning and coding activities, builds persistent long-term memory, analyzes developer growth, identifies knowledge gaps, and provides personalized learning and career recommendations from a single platform.
